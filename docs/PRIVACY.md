# RavnDock Privacy

RavnDock processes prompts, attached files, and knowledge-base documents locally on your Mac. The app does not intentionally upload document contents or prompts to a cloud service.

Internet access is used only when you approve a model or supporting software download. Local models are provided through Ollama, and your selected knowledge-base folder remains the source of truth.

You can remove a knowledge base, delete local models, or reset RavnDock data from the app. Removing a knowledge base does not delete your original documents.
