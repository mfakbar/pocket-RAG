# RavnDock Privacy Policy Draft

Last updated: July 28, 2026

RavnDock is designed to process prompts and selected documents locally on your Mac.

## Data processing

RavnDock reads only folders that you choose. Document text, prompts, embeddings,
search indexes, chat history, settings, and performance measurements remain on your
Mac. RavnDock does not intentionally transmit them to the developer or to an
analytics, advertising, or tracking service.

## Internet access

Internet access is used only for downloads that you approve, such as local AI model
files and application resources. Chatting with synchronized documents is designed to
work locally after setup.

## Data collection

RavnDock does not intentionally collect personal data, analytics, diagnostics, or
advertising identifiers. Apple may process App Store purchase and download data under
Apple's own policies.

## Your controls

You can remove chats, models, synchronized indexes, and RavnDock application data
from your Mac. Removing a knowledge base does not modify the original documents in
the folder you selected.
