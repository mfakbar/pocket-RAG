RavnDock · Retrieval-augmented generation

What happens behind the scenes

RAG lets a local chat model answer with evidence from your files. RavnDock first prepares a searchable local index, then retrieves the most useful passages for each question.

Runs locally on this Mac
1 · Prepare

Read files, preserve locations, split useful passages, and build a local index.

2 · Retrieve

Search by meaning and exact terms, then focus the best evidence for the question.

3 · Generate + verify

Stream a grounded answer, check its citations, and link claims back to source files.
